Games like Adventure Capitalist, with a parody political twist

If you like idle business games, try Star-Spangled Tycoon. It is free in your browser, and it swaps the corporate empire for a political campaign.

📱 Free on the App Store▶ Or play in your browser

If you are looking for games like Adventure Capitalist, you are probably after the idle business loop: buy a business, let it earn, then spend the money on the next one. Star-Spangled Tycoon uses that same loop. You tap to start, your businesses earn while you are away, and each purchase makes the next one more reachable. If you already enjoy the genre, the rhythm will feel familiar within a few minutes.

The difference is the setting. Instead of building a company, you are a parody politician trying to win an election. The businesses are American-themed, from a flag stand to a fireworks factory, and the progress system is built around elections. When you hold an election you reset your progress in exchange for a lasting bonus. The full game has 5 worlds and 50 businesses, and each world adds a new mechanic to the basic loop.

The game is a parody. Its characters, a Cabinet of cartoon politicians, are fictional, and its jokes are about politics in general rather than about any real person or party. Along with the core loop, there are achievements and a rotating calendar of 31 limited events. If you like the business loop but want a different theme, this is a fair place to start.

Star-Spangled Tycoon is free in the browser and needs no download. The browser version is supported by ads at natural breaks, and some rewards are unlocked by a short ad. The iPhone and iPad app is also free on the App Store, with optional purchases. Progress in the browser is saved on that device, and the settings menu can export a save if you want a backup.

Every idle game asks for patience, and this one is no exception. The first few minutes are simple, and the decisions get more interesting as the numbers grow. Give it a session or two. If the business loop is what you enjoy, you will know quickly. If it is not, you will have lost very little. The game also has a Cabinet to meet, a campaign to run and a calendar of events to follow, so there is more to see than the business loop alone. Expect the first session to be about learning the buttons and the second to be about planning your purchases. The political frame is the main difference, so the rest of the decisions will feel familiar. If the business loop is what you enjoy, most of your decisions will come down to timing.

Frequently asked questions

Is Star-Spangled Tycoon similar to Adventure Capitalist?

Both are idle business games where you buy businesses, let them earn and spend the money on upgrades. Star-Spangled Tycoon has a political parody theme and an election reset.

Is it free to play?

Yes. The browser version at the play page is free and supported by ads. The iPhone and iPad app is free on the App Store.

Do I have to download it?

No, not for the browser version. The iPhone and iPad app is the only download.

What makes it different from other idle business games?

The theme. You play a parody politician, the businesses have an American political flavour, and progress is built around elections and a Cabinet of cartoon characters.
