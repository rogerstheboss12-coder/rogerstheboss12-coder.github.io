Star-Spangled Tycoon FAQ

Free on the App Store · or play in your browser

Is Star-Spangled Tycoon free?

Yes. It is free to download on iPhone and iPad and free to play in a web browser. In-app purchases are optional, and nothing is required to play.

What platforms is it on?

iPhone and iPad through the App Store, and in any modern web browser at /play/. There is no Android release yet.

Can I play it at school? Is it unblocked?

The web version at /play/ needs no download or install. We cannot tell you whether your school's filter allows it, because each school sets its own filter.

Does it work on a Chromebook?

Yes. The web version at /play/ runs in Chrome on a Chromebook. The App Store app is for iPhone and iPad.

Is it real politics?

No. It is a lighthearted parody. The politicians are historical figures or fictional characters with parody names, and no endorsement is implied.

How long does it take to beat?

There is no ending and no final credits. The game has 5 worlds, 50 businesses and 689 achievements, and the Constitutional Convention resets your empire for permanent perks. You set your own pace.

Is it available in Spanish?

Yes. English and Spanish are built in, including the text of the rotating events.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your businesses keep earning while the app is closed, and the offline earnings are added when you come back.

Does it have ads?

The iPhone and iPad app has no banner or full-screen ads. Watching a short rewarded video is optional and gives a bonus. The web version at /play/ shows ads, and its rewards are only granted after an ad or a short house ad finishes.

Are there in-app purchases?

Yes, and they are optional. There are 5 Liberty Bucks packs, and everything in the game can also be earned by playing.

What are the rotating events?

A new 3-day event starts every three days. Each event has its own businesses, currency and reward tiers, which can include permanent Liberty Badges. Events are written in English and Spanish.

What are leagues?

Every week a rival power, such as the Grand Duchy of Blandovia, races you to the stars. Beat them by Monday to climb from Bronze to the Galactic League.

Does it have Game Center?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad. Game Center leaderboards and achievements are included.

What are the weekly Showdowns?

Every Saturday a new monster appears in a 24-hour Weekly Showdown. You can climb the Showdown and event leaderboards.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device. On iPhone and iPad it also uses iCloud sync and on-device backups. In the web version, the save is kept in your browser for that site address.

Who made it?

Star-Spangled Tycoon was made by Paydown Developing, a one-person independent studio.

How do I contact the developer?

Use the support page at /star-spangled-tycoon/support.html.

Does it collect personal data?

See the privacy policy at /star-spangled-tycoon/privacy.html for what is collected and why.
