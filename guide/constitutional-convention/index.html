Constitutional Convention: progression guide

The Convention is the second prestige layer. It opens once Known Universe is unlocked.

Recommended order

  1. Start in Earth with Flag Stand, the cheapest business.
  2. Earn 1.00×1015 Dollars to unlock Solar System ("Fund the Moonshot").
  3. Earn 1.00×1016 Moon Dollars to unlock Milky Way ("Build the Warp Gate").
  4. Earn 1.00×1017 Galactic Credits to unlock Known Universe ("Annex the Universe").
  5. Earn 1.00×1018 Omnibucks to unlock The Multiverse ("Open the Multiverse Rift").
  6. Once Known Universe is unlocked, the Convention is open.

Amendments

Amendments = floor((Σ log10(lifetime) ÷ 60)²). Each amendment multiplies all profit by ×1.25 and adds 0.5% to prestige effectiveness.

Work through the worlds in the order above. Business numbers are in the world guides.
