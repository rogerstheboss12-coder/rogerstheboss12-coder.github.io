Privacy Policy

Star-Spangled Tycoon · Effective date: September 26, 2026

Star-Spangled Tycoon ("the Game") is an idle game you play on your own, with optional leaderboards. We designed it to collect as little information as possible. This policy explains what data is involved and why.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game has no accounts or logins of its own. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information. Leaderboards use a generated display name (see section 7b), never your real name.

2. Your game progress

Your save file (cash, businesses, settings, achievements) is stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled on your device, a copy is also stored in your private iCloud account using Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage so progress can move between your devices. We cannot access this data.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The Game receives only a transaction confirmation from Apple so it can deliver your item.

4. Advertising

If you choose to watch an optional rewarded video (for example, to double a boost), the ad is served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks, as well as coarse location derived from your IP address, to serve and measure ads. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Owners of the Founding Fathers Pack never see ads. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Time verification

To prevent offline earnings from being exploited by changing the device clock, the Game reads the current time from a public Apple web server (www.apple.com). This request contains no personal or game data. As with any internet request, Apple's server sees your IP address.

6. Gameplay statistics

The Game may record anonymous gameplay events (for example "tutorial finished" or "world unlocked") tied to a random install identifier that is not linked to you, your device identifiers or any account. These statistics are used only to balance and improve the Game and are never sold or used for advertising.

7. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the Game schedules notifications locally on your device. No push-notification tokens are sent to us.

7a. Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which parts of the Game players enjoy and where they get stuck, the Game sends anonymous gameplay events (for example "mission completed" or "tutorial step 3") to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU. Events include the app version, your device language and a random install identifier that is hashed (SHA-256) before it leaves your device. They contain no name, email, advertising identifier, location or other personal information, and are not used for advertising or tracking. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage stats.

7b. Leaderboards and Game Center

Event and Showdown leaderboards. To rank you against other players in groups of up to 100, the Game sends our leaderboard server a random player identifier created by the Game, a display name generated from a fixed list of words (for example "Governor Otis of New Ohio"; you can pick a new one in Settings), which leaderboard it is for, and your score on it. Other players in your group can see your display name and score. The server does not store your IP address, device identifiers or any account information, and deletes leaderboard entries after 30 days. When you are offline, or if the server is unavailable, the Game shows computer-controlled rivals instead (marked 🤖), and nothing is sent.

Campaign votes and invite codes. If you vote in Campaign HQ, the Game sends our server the same random player identifier and your choice of candidate, so there is one vote per player. Only vote totals are ever shown. If you use invite codes, the server stores your code, your random player identifier, and which code a new player entered, so it can count friends and pay rewards once. The browser demo sends a separate random identifier when you vote. None of this is linked to your identity.

Game Center. If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the Game reports your scores on a few leaderboards and some achievements to Apple. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive your Game Center identity.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your choices

You can decline tracking when iOS asks, change ad consent in Settings, turn reminders and anonymous usage stats off, and erase all progress in Settings → Reset everything. Deleting the app removes local data; iCloud data can be removed in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
