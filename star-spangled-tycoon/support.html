Star-Spangled Tycoon Support

Need help, found a bug, or have a purchase problem? Email us at PaydownDeveloping@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

My purchase didn't arrive. Open Store → Get Liberty Bucks → Restore purchases. Purchases are also redelivered automatically the next time you open the game.

How do I move my progress to a new device? Progress syncs through iCloud when you're signed in with the same Apple ID. You can also use More → Settings → Export / Import.

Why didn't I earn offline money? If your device clock was moved backwards, offline earnings are skipped. If you were offline with no internet connection, earnings are limited to 8 hours until the time can be verified.
