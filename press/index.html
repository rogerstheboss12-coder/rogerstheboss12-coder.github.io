Description

Star-Spangled Tycoon is a lighthearted parody idle tycoon game. Start with a Flag Stand and build an empire across 5 worlds and 50 businesses, hire a cabinet of parody presidents to run them, and keep earning while you are away. The politicians are historical figures or fictional characters with parody names; no endorsement is implied.

Features

  • 50 businesses across 5 worlds
  • A cabinet of parody presidents who run your businesses
  • 689 achievements and Game Center leaderboards
  • Weekly Showdown boss battles and Space Race leagues
  • A new 3-day event every three days
  • Offline earnings and iCloud sync on iPhone and iPad
  • English and Spanish
